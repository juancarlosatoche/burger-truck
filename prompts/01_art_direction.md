# Stage 1: Art direction mockups

**Tool:** any image generator (ChatGPT, Gemini, FAL, Midjourney…)
**Save to:** `reference/mockups/mockup_<letter>_<style>.png`

## How to use

1. Paste the **shared scene** and **one style** together as one prompt.
2. Generate all 4 styles. Generate 2–3 variations of each if you can.
3. Pick a favorite, or two to blend. Note the choice in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

Use 16:9 (landscape) so it matches the game's screen.

## Shared scene (paste first)

> Video game screenshot, first-person view from inside a small burger food truck, standing behind the stainless steel counter, looking out through the open service window. On the counter: a plate with a half-built burger, ketchup and mustard bottles, a small computer screen. Outside, at dusk: three cartoon customers waiting in line, the first one with a speech bubble showing a burger order; a small plaza with two umbrella tables, warm string lights between poles, a street lamp, a few trees, and city buildings with lit windows in the background. Sky shifting from orange sunset to deep blue. Cozy, inviting, family-friendly. No text or UI except the speech bubble.

## Styles (add one)

**A. Storybook painterly**
> Style: hand-painted storybook look, soft brush textures, warm golden rim lighting, gentle ambient occlusion, slightly exaggerated rounded shapes, rich but not oversaturated colors.

**B. Soft clay / toy diorama**
> Style: everything looks made of soft matte clay and plastic toys, rounded edges everywhere, subtle fingerprints and surface texture, tilt-shift depth of field, soft global illumination.

**C. Cel-shaded cartoon**
> Style: cel-shaded cartoon, clean bold outlines, flat color bands with 2–3 shading steps, vivid sunset palette, crisp shadows, expressive characters with big heads.

**D. Cozy low-poly with glow**
> Style: stylized low-poly 3D, faceted shapes with smooth gradient lighting, strong bloom on the string lights and sign, volumetric warm light, soft fog, cozy sunset mood.
